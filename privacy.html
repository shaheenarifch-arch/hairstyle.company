Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information hairstyle.company ("we", "us") collects when you use hairstyle.company, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe using the email form on this site, we collect your email address and the page you signed up from. We use it only to send you The Hair Letter (new arrivals, brand sales and how-tos). Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Messages: if you email or call us, we receive your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us.
  • Technical information: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations.
  • No accounts or payments: you do not need an account to use this site, and we never collect payment details. Purchases happen on the official brand stores and on Amazon.

How we use information

  • To send the newsletter to people who subscribed.
  • To reply to your messages and requests.
  • To correct or improve our content and keep the site working and secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Cookies and affiliate links

Many links on this site are affiliate links, through the Awin affiliate network and the Amazon Associates program. When you click one, you leave our site. The affiliate network, the brand or Amazon may set cookies or use similar technologies to record that you came from us and to process your order. Their own privacy policies apply to that data. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers of our own on this site.

Third-party content and links

Product images and links come from the official stores of UNice, Zlike, Allove Hair, Jumy Bee and other partners, and some photos come from Unsplash, so your browser may load them directly from those providers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites, so please read their policies before sharing personal information.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Changes
