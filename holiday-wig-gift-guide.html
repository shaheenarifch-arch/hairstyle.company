Choosing a wig as a gift

A wig is personal, so a little preparation goes a long way. These three details matter most:

  • Cap size. Most wigs list an average cap size and many have adjustable straps, but head sizes vary. Check the size chart on the product page, and if you can, ask the recipient for their head circumference.
  • Lace type. HD lace is the thinnest and blends with more skin tones; transparent lace is slightly thicker and tougher. See our HD vs transparent lace guide.
  • Density. About 150% looks light and natural, 180% is the popular full everyday look, and 250% or more is very full. See our density and length guide.

Tip: if you are not sure about size, texture or color, ask the recipient for their measurements and preferences, or choose a hair-care gift or a retailer gift card if the retailer offers one. That avoids sizing problems entirely.

Frequently asked questions

What is the safest wig-related gift?

Hair-care products, such as a shampoo and conditioner set or a hair mask, are the lowest-risk choice because they need no cap size, color or length. A retailer gift card, if the retailer offers one, is another safe option.

Is it a good idea to give a wig as a surprise?

Only if you know the recipient’s preferences. Wigs depend on cap size, texture, color, length and lace type, so it helps to ask for these details or to gift a hair-care set instead.

What is the difference between HD lace and transparent lace?

HD lace is the thinnest and blends with more skin tones. Transparent lace is slightly thicker and more durable. Both are used for natural-looking hairlines.

Which wig density should I choose?

Around 150% looks light and natural, 180% is the popular full everyday look, and 250% or more is very full and suits long lengths and special occasions.

Can a wig gift be returned?
