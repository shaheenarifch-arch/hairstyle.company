Questions, answered

What is hairstyle.company?

hairstyle.company is an independent edit of human hair wigs and hair care from four official brand stores: UNice, Zlike, Allove Hair and Jumy Bee. We explain the specs in plain English and link you straight to the brand to buy.

What is the best wig for beginners?

A 7x5 glueless wig with pre-cut HD lace, pre-plucked hairline and bleached knots. It goes on in minutes with an elastic band, with no glue. Read our beginner guide.

HD lace or transparent lace?

HD lace is the thinnest and most invisible, great for close-ups. Transparent lace is slightly thicker and more durable. See HD vs transparent lace.

What density should I choose?

180% is the popular full, natural look. Choose 150% for lightweight everyday wear, or 250-300% for glam volume. See our density guide.

Are these real human hair wigs?

The wigs we feature are listed by the brands as 100% human hair (many as virgin hair), so they can be washed, heat-styled and, in many cases, colored. Always check the individual listing.

Do you earn money from links?

Yes. We may earn a commission when you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the price. Read our disclosure.
